Connect the free AI (one-off, 2 minutes)

TradeTalk writes descriptions with Google's Gemini AI. A Gemini key is free and only needs a Google account.

  1. Open aistudio.google.com/apikey and sign in with Google.
  2. Press Create API key and copy it.
  3. Paste it below and press Save key.

The key stays on this device only, in this browser. Photos go straight from your browser to Google to be described; nothing is stored on a TradeTalk server. On Google's free tier, Google may use what you send to improve its products. If your photos are confidential, turn on billing for the key in Google AI Studio (a few cents per hundred photos) so they aren't used.
